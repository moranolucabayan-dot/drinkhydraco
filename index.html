The formula

What’s in the powder

Nine real ingredients. Tap any one.

  1. Coconut water powderThe largest share +

    What it’s forThe base of the mix.

    Why it helpsCoconut water is naturally rich in potassium, which helps your body hold on to the water you drink instead of passing straight through.

  2. Organic cane sugar10–15% +

    What it’s forA small amount of real sugar — 2 g.

    Why it helpsA little sugar helps your body absorb the water and salts faster than plain water does. Just enough to do that, not enough to taste like a soda.

  3. Apple powder10–15% +

    What it’s forFlavour and natural fruit tartness.

    Why it helpsRounds out the taste so the drink isn’t sharp, and brings a little more potassium with it.

  4. Tart cherry powder10–15% +

    What it’s forThe first of the two recovery fruits.

    Why it helpsTart cherries are one of the most studied fruits for recovery — they’re rich in natural antioxidants and are linked to less muscle soreness after hard exercise.

  5. Lemon juice powder10–15% +

    What it’s forThe lemonade side of the flavour.

    Why it helpsKeeps the drink bright and fresh rather than sweet, and it’s a natural source of vitamin C.

  6. Pomegranate extract powder5–10% +

    What it’s forThe second recovery fruit.

    Why it helpsAnother fruit packed with natural antioxidants, studied alongside tart cherry for helping muscles bounce back after training.

  7. Monk fruit extract5–10% +

    What it’s forSweetness without the sugar.

    Why it helpsMonk fruit is a natural fruit sweetener — it’s how the drink tastes sweet with only 2 g of real sugar and no artificial sweeteners.

  8. Pumpkin seed extract powderUnder 5% +

    What it’s forA plant source of magnesium.

    Why it helpsMagnesium is the mineral muscles need to relax again after they’ve worked — and most people don’t get enough of it.

  9. Pink Himalayan saltUnder 5% +

    What it’s forWhere the sodium comes from.

    Why it helpsSalt is what you lose most of when you sweat — we’ve bumped it up a little so each stick puts back more of it. Still a gentle amount, not a salt bomb.

Amounts are rounded and shown as ranges — the full recipe is ours, but nothing in it is a secret ingredient. Every one is on the pack.

Founding story

I was seventeen, training in Bethesda, Maryland, and looking for something to drink after practice that actually helped me recover.

The search

Everything on the shelf was built to hydrate you during a workout. I wanted the opposite — something for the hours after.

What was missing

The options were high in sugar, full of additives, and expensive — and the fruits with real recovery research, tart cherry and pomegranate, were barely in any of them.

The build

So I made Restore: low sugar, a gentle electrolyte base, and meaningful amounts of both fruits. Recovery as a daily habit, not an afterthought.

Luca Bayan Morano Hydraco founder

Good to know

Why is everything a pre-order?

Restore is produced in small batches and supply is limited. Rather than show stock we don't have, every order is placed against an upcoming batch. You'll receive a confirmation email with your ship date once your order is allocated.

How do I pay?

By Zelle, sent from your bank's app to the number shown in the order panel. Put your order reference in the memo so we can match the payment to your order. We never collect or store card details on this site.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

Delivery is free in New York City, and in Maryland, D.C. and Virginia within roughly an hour's drive of our Maryland office. If you're outside that area, go ahead and place the order — we'll email you a delivery quote and a revised total before you send any payment. Don't Zelle us until that's confirmed.

When will my order arrive?

Orders are fulfilled in the sequence they're received, once the batch is complete. Your confirmation email carries the specific date for your order.

How do I use it?

Serving instructions are printed on each stick packet. One stick pack is one serving.

How should I store it?
