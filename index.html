FAQ

Keep it clean, confident, and adult-friendly.

How do I use Restore?

Mix 1 stick with ~16 oz of cool or warm water. Drink after activity, or use daily as a recovery routine.

Is it okay to eat it dry?

It’s designed to be mixed with water for proper hydration. Dry use won’t hydrate you the same way and may taste intense—water is part of the system.

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s a nutrition product. If pregnant, nursing, taking medications, or managing a condition, talk to a clinician.
