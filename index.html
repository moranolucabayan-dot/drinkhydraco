Active formula

What’s in the powder — and what it delivers

Nine real ingredients, each earning its place. Tap any one for the mechanism behind it, then scroll down for exactly what one stick delivers.

  1. Coconut water powderThe largest share +

    What it’s forThe base of the mix.

    Why it helpsCoconut water is naturally rich in potassium, which helps your body hold on to the water you drink instead of passing straight through.

  2. Organic cane sugar10–15% +

    What it’s forA small amount of real sugar — 2 g.

    Why it helpsA little sugar helps your body absorb the water and salts faster than plain water does. Just enough to do that, not enough to taste like a soda.

  3. Apple powder10–15% +

    What it’s forFlavour and natural fruit tartness.

    Why it helpsRounds out the taste so the drink isn’t sharp, and brings a little more potassium with it.

  4. Tart cherry powder10–15% +

    What it’s forThe first of the two recovery fruits.

    Why it helpsTart cherry is one of the most studied fruits for recovery: its natural antioxidants target the oxidative stress hard training leaves behind, and it’s linked in research to measurably less delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) — the deep ache that peaks a day or two after a hard session.

    A 2021 meta-analysis pooling 14 trials found tart cherry gave a small reduction in muscle soreness and a moderate improvement in how quickly strength and power came back after strenuous exercise. Hill et al., 2021, International Journal of Sport Nutrition and Exercise Metabolism

    In a small placebo-controlled trial of 10 people, a week of Montmorency tart cherry concentrate before hard eccentric leg exercise helped muscle strength recover faster in the days afterwards. Wangdi et al., 2022, Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise

  5. Lemon juice powder10–15% +

    What it’s forThe lemonade side of the flavour.

    Why it helpsKeeps the drink bright and fresh rather than sweet, and it’s a natural source of vitamin C.

  6. Pomegranate extract powder5–10% +

    What it’s forThe second recovery fruit.

    Why it helpsPomegranate’s polyphenols are studied alongside tart cherry for the same reason: they help neutralize post-exercise oxidative stress, which is why they’re studied for muscle recovery and DOMS.

    A systematic review of the research concluded pomegranate has the potential to deliver antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects during and after exercise, and to improve endurance. Ammar et al., 2018, British Journal of Nutrition

    The evidence is still mixed, though: a 2025 meta-analysis of 10 trials found pomegranate lowered one blood marker of muscle damage (LDH) right after exercise, but didn’t significantly change muscle soreness. Belyani et al., 2025, Current Developments in Nutrition

  7. Monk fruit extract5–10% +

    What it’s forSweetness without the sugar.

    Why it helpsMonk fruit is a natural fruit sweetener — it’s how the drink tastes sweet with only 2 g of real sugar and no artificial sweeteners.

  8. Pumpkin seed extract powderUnder 5% +

    What it’s forA plant source of magnesium.

    Why it helpsMagnesium is the mineral muscles need to relax again after they’ve worked — and most people don’t get enough of it.

  9. Pink Himalayan saltUnder 5% +

    What it’s forWhere the sodium comes from.

    Why it helpsSalt is what you lose most of when you sweat — we’ve bumped it up a little so each stick puts back more of it. Still a gentle amount, not a salt bomb.

Founding story

I was seventeen, training in Bethesda, Maryland, and looking for something to drink after practice that actually helped me recover.

The search

Built for after, not duringEverything on the shelf was built to hydrate you during a workout. I wanted the opposite — something for the hours after.

What was missing

Sugar, additives — barely any real cherry or pomegranateThe options were high in sugar, full of additives, and expensive — and the fruits with real recovery research, tart cherry and pomegranate, were barely in any of them.

The build

So I made RestoreLow sugar, a gentle electrolyte base, and meaningful amounts of both fruits. Recovery as a daily habit, not an afterthought.

Luca Bayan Morano Hydraco founder
